Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about us today!

What types of properties do you list?
We list residential, commercial, and rental properties, including houses, apartments, condos, land, and office spaces.
What fees are involved in selling a home?
Typical selling costs include agent commissions (10%).
Do you help with rentals and property management?
Yes, we manage rentals and full property management packages with transparent fees. For more details, login to you account Menu.
How often are listings updated?
Our listings are updated daily to ensure you have the most accurate information on availability and pricing.
How do I make an offer/negotiate on a property?
You can submit an offer/negotiate directly by bidding through our website and we will reach out to you.
How do I list my property?
Click on “Sell Your Property” and complete the listing form with property details, photo, and pricing. Our team may review the details.
Are owners verified?
Yes, all owners are verified to ensure legitimacy. Verified owners have a tag attached to them.
Do I need an account to browse listings?
Yes, creating an account allows you to get the ultimate experience to you as our esteemed customer.
How do I contact customer support?
You can reach our support team via the “Contact Us” page or by calling our helpline listed on the website.
